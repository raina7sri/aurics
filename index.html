Why I Built the AI Visibility Scorecard

Buyers increasingly discover products through AI assistants — but traditional marketing and SEO tools don't measure how those assistants recommend, mention, or cite a brand. So I build the tools that do, in the open, and test them on real products and services.

The AI Visibility Scorecard came out of exactly that: I rebuilt a brand's positioning, website, and AEO from the ground up, then needed a way to prove it was actually surfacing in AI search, but wasn't committed to spending hundreds on it yet. So it became a strategy — that shipped.
